# Engineering Principles

## Principle 1

Never reinvent solved problems.

---

## Principle 2

Local First.

The user's computer should perform work whenever possible.

---

## Principle 3

Open Source First.

Always search for mature open-source projects before writing custom code.

---

## Principle 4

Everything is Modular.

Every capability must be a Tool.

---

## Principle 5

The Brain never performs work.

The Brain only decides who performs work.

---

## Principle 6

Memory is permanent.

Knowledge is cumulative.

Context is continuous.

---

## Principle 7

Everything must be documented.

No hidden knowledge.

---

## Principle 8

One AI should be replaceable by another AI without losing project knowledge.
