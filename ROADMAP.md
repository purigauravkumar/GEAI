# GEAI Master Roadmap

## Vision

GEAI (General Effective Artificial Intelligence) is a persistent AI system that can:

* Remember information
* Organize projects
* Learn from documents
* Learn from websites
* Build its own knowledge base
* Search its knowledge efficiently
* Continuously acquire knowledge
* Eventually operate as an autonomous search and reasoning engine

The long-term goal is to create a practical General Effective Artificial Intelligence focused on effectiveness, memory, knowledge acquisition, and reasoning.
